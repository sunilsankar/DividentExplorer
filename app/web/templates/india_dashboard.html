About Indian Dividend Stocks & Exchange Ingestion

Exchanges: The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) uses ticker suffix .NS (e.g., ITC.NS, TCS.NS), while the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) uses suffix .BO (e.g., ITC.BO).

Currency: All prices, dividends, and cash flows for Indian stocks are tracked in Indian Rupees (₹ / INR).

High Yields & PSUs: Indian Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) such as Coal India, REC, PFC, and Power Grid frequently pay government-mandated high dividends (6%–12% yields). Quality scores evaluate payout sustainability to differentiate robust cash flows from dividend traps.
