How Ingestion Works & Troubleshooting Guide

• Worker Process: Syncing runs in a detached background worker. Start it with python -m app.sync.worker.

• Rate Limits & Throttling: Upstream Yahoo requests include conservative delays and jitter. If rate-limited (HTTP 429), the worker automatically pauses and backs off.

• Job Retries: Transient network errors retry up to 5 times with exponential backoff. Retrying jobs wait in the queue until their scheduled backoff elapses.

• Stuck Locks: If a worker terminates abruptly while a job is running, the lock will be detected as stale after 5 minutes and can be reaped automatically or via the Reap Stuck Jobs button.
